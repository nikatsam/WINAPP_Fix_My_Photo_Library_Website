Product behavior summary · 14 October 2026

What the app reads

When you choose folders, the app enumerates supported photo files and reads file metadata or image bytes needed for the requested check. It uses SHA-256 for content comparisons and a Windows image decoder for supported-image checks. It may read EXIF capture-date fields to show a date preview.

Cloud/offline placeholders, inaccessible folders and reparse-point paths are not silently traversed. A scan that cannot finish is reported as incomplete.

Network and uploads

The app’s photo-processing code does not send photo content, filenames, hashes or reports to a remote service. It does not implement cloud storage or analytics. Windows, the Microsoft Store and other operating-system services may have their own network behavior unrelated to photo processing; those platform services are not controlled by this application.

Reports, history and journals

CSV and JSON reports are written only to a folder you select. The local job-history file stores summary counts and outcomes without photo paths. A separate local action journal retains paths and hashes needed for undo, quarantine recovery and restore. Clearing job history does not erase an action journal or a report you exported.

Date-corrected copies and quarantine files go to folders you select. Source images are not silently overwritten or permanently deleted by the duplicate workflow.

Your choices

You select the folders, review proposed changes and confirm copy or quarantine operations. You can clear the summary history in the app. To remove reports or action journals, use Windows File Explorer and the documented app-data location after the final release instructions are published.

Before the Store release

This summary must be reviewed by the product owner and aligned with the final publisher identity, support contact, package behavior and Microsoft Store listing. The full legal policy and contact details will be published before the app is offered for purchase.
